Privacy Policy — Parhai Companion

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Last updated: 24 October 2026
App: Parhai Companion (package com.shan.parhaicompanion)
Developer: Shan Ali Mastoi

Parhai Companion is an offline-first study app for students from Nursery/KG through Grade 12. It is designed so that children can learn without their personal information ever leaving their device.

In short: we do not collect, store, or share your personal information. All learning data stays on your device. Google services are involved only for advertising in the app's older-student sections (Dictionary, General Knowledge, and Grade 6+) and, only if you choose to use a score challenge, for the anonymous challenge sync explained in §5.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. The app:

  • requires no account, sign-up, or login;
  • asks for no name, email, phone number, or address;
  • sends no usage analytics to any server;
  • uses an anonymous challenge ID only if you choose to send or open a Beat My Score challenge (see §5).

2. Information that stays on your device

The following are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Study progress — quiz scores, flashcard reviews, study-planner entries, and favourites;
  • App settings — theme, board selection, notification reminders;
  • Profile photo — if you choose one from your gallery, it is used only inside the app;
  • Scanner/camera images — pictures taken with the in-app scanner (e.g. Scan & Learn) are processed on your device and never uploaded;
  • Parent Corner settings — protected by a parent gate and stored locally.

3. Permissions the app may request

Each permission is used only on your device, for its stated purpose:

  • Camera — Scan & Learn (images processed on-device, never uploaded);
  • Photos / storage — choosing a profile picture from your gallery;
  • Notifications — study reminders you set in the Planner;
  • Internet — only for (a) showing banner ads in the ad-supported sections, (b) sending or opening a Beat My Score challenge if you choose to use that feature (see §5). All lessons, quizzes, and flashcards work fully offline.

4. Advertising

  • Banner ads appear only in the Dictionary, General Knowledge, and Grade 6+ sections.
  • Nursery/KG through Grade 5 are completely ad-free, always.
  • Ads are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) with child-directed treatment enabled and the maximum ad content rating set to G. No personalized ads are shown to children.
  • Google may collect device identifiers to serve these ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by us — we do not receive any of that data.

5. Sharing features

Features such as Beat My Score open your phone's own share menu. Anything you share goes to the app you choose (e.g. WhatsApp, Facebook) — never to us.

If you send or open a Beat My Score challenge, the app uses Google Firebase anonymous sign-in so the other player can see the challenge result. Only the challenge ID, the quiz reference, both players' scores, and the completion state are synced. No name, email, phone number, photo, contacts, or messages are collected or synced for challenges.

6. Children

The app is made for children and treats all users as children for advertising purposes (child-directed ad flags are set, as described in §4). Because we collect no personal information, there is no children's data to review or delete. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us at the address below.

7. Data retention and your rights

Apart from the anonymous challenge records described in §5 — which contain no directly identifying information — we hold no personal information about you on any server, and we never sell data. To erase everything the app stored on your phone, simply uninstall the app — all on-device data is removed with it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and linked from the app and the Play Store listing. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
